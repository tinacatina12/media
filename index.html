Support via Airtel Uganda

Follow these simple steps to send your support through Airtel Money:

  1. Dial *185# on your Airtel line
  2. Select "Send Money"
  3. Select "To Airtel Number"
  4. Enter our number: 0759970315
  5. Enter the amount you wish to send
  6. Enter your PIN to confirm the transaction
  7. You will receive a confirmation message
Transaction fees may apply based on your Airtel Money tariff.

Support via MTN Uganda

Follow these simple steps to send your support through MTN Mobile Money:

  1. Dial *165# on your MTN line
  2. Select "Send Money"
  3. Select "MTN Mobile Money"
  4. Enter our number: 0759970315
  5. Enter the amount you wish to send
  6. Enter your PIN to confirm the transaction
  7. You will receive a confirmation message
Transaction fees may apply based on your MTN Mobile Money tariff.
